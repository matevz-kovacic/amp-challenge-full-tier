# Intervention and reproducibility disclosure

AI assistants helped write research code, inspect methods and evidence, and implement deterministic consolidation and packaging. Human direction fixed task scope, resource constraints, participation preparation and disclosure boundaries. No LLM is called during generation, scoring-table replay or selection. Raw prompts, agent traces and the external research framework are not runtime dependencies.

Ten completed runs were chosen by completion status, not by final consensus contribution. Their generation and model-selection decisions predate consolidation. Common evaluators and their family weights were frozen before examining new consensus winners. All 499,998 candidates received the same panel; source ranking and repeated source occurrence provide no additional votes. Manual peptide edits, hand labeling of hidden test data, and per-sequence human selection were not used in consolidation.

The quality index, MMR lambda, screen size, cluster coordinates/count and allocation policy are explicit local choices. Existing predictor scores were reused only after sequence/hash identity checks; missing values were scored with the same frozen implementation. No new training, source-pool design experiment or aggregation-method search occurred during consolidation or submission preparation.

Default source seeds are fixed: sources01/03/04/07/09 use 0; sources02/06/08 use 20260908; source05 uses 50317; source10 uses 271828. Every source's ordered output is checked, including pools needed to define the ranking population. Changing a source, weight, seed, score, rank or selection artifact requires an explicit new version. The submitted sequence identities and byte hashes remain frozen.

Chemistry is the intended representation: canonical L amino acids, N-to-C, linear, free unmodified termini. No peptide has been synthesized or experimentally validated by this preparation. Content validity and repeatable computation do not establish benchmark qualification, potency, safety or co-authorship eligibility.
